# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Syllabus2Calendar

## Target Audience
College and high school students seeking to eliminate manual calendar entry and avoid deadline clutter.

## User Persona
- **Name:** Alex (Student)
- **Problem:** Receives 5+ syllabi at the start of the semester; manual calendar entry takes hours and often misses prep milestones.
- **Goal:** Wants a 1-click solution to upload course dates and get a clean timeline in Apple Calendar or Google Calendar.

## Key Requirements & Acceptance Criteria
1. **Input Interface:** Text area for syllabus pasting with sample text generator for fast testing.
2. **Parsing Engine:** Instant regex and keyword identification for assignments, quizzes, and exams.
3. **Interactive Timeline:** Progress tracking with real-time percentage completion metrics.
4. **Export Engine:** Formatted `.ics` file generation respecting standard RFC 5545 calendar specifications.
5. **Responsiveness:** Liquid-glass sapphire dark UI accessible across mobile and desktop viewports.
